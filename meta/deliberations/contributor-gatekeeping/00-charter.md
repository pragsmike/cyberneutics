# Charter: Contributor Gatekeeping Problem

The committee will deliberate on how to make the Cyberneutics repository unambiguously welcoming to exploratory contributors while preserving the structural conventions that agents and structured contributors depend on. The brief identifies specific files, passages, and a concrete failure case (a contributor who self-selected out). The committee should produce actionable file-level recommendations, not abstract principles.
