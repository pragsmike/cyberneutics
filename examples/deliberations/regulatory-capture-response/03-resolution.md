# Resolution: Responding to Regulatory Capture Coercion

## Decision

Adopt a phased branching strategy:

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2):** All reversible actions in parallel — documentation, outside counsel, governance charter adoption, deadline extension request, internal alignment integrity review, peer outreach.

**Phase 2 (Weeks 2-8):** Intelligence-informed preparation — legal opinion and TRO assessment, agency personnel mapping, coalition formalization, financial resilience assessment.

**Phase 3 (Week 8):** Board decision point on escalation path, informed by evidence quality, coalition status, and legal position. Binding, not advisory.

**Contingency (if extension denied):** Board activates charter escalation clause within 48 hours. Path depends on evidence quality — strong evidence enables TRO and possible exposure; weak evidence forces the unresolved Maya/Frankie choice.

## Rationale

The scenario set reveals that the most dangerous outcome (Slow Anneal) is the organizational default — it requires no decisions, only non-decisions. Every other scenario requires deliberate action. The strategy therefore prioritizes actions that prevent the default while preserving flexibility on which non-default path to pursue.

The governance charter is the structural backbone: it makes compliance *visible* as a governance event rather than a quiet business decision. The internal alignment integrity review is the organizational immune system: it creates the feedback loop that detects cumulative erosion. The Week 8 decision point is the commitment mechanism that prevents indefinite deferral.

## Unresolved

If the worst case materializes (extension denied, weak evidence, no coalition), the committee has a genuine values disagreement. Maya argues for minimal tactical compliance to preserve organizational capacity. Frankie argues for accelerated exposure to preserve mission integrity. Both positions are coherent. The board decides.

## Unanimous with Conditions

All five members voted YES. Conditions: worst-case fallback remains on the table (Maya); Week 8 decision point is binding (Frankie); governance charter is adopted, not just discussed (Joe); legal assessment obtained in Weeks 1-2 (Vic); internal alignment review established with direct board reporting (Tammy).
