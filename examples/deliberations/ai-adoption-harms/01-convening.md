# Convening: AI Engagement Framework Deliberation

**Date**: 2026-03-27

**Selection strategy**: Standard roster from agent/roster.md + special-purpose character Dr. Reva from artifacts/character-skeptic-ai-reva.md.

**Rationale**: This deliberation requires the standard analytical diversity (political realism, values, institutional memory, evidence, systems) plus a structurally distinct voice: principled opposition to the entire premise of AI engagement. The standard roster optimizes *within* the assumption that AI will be used; Reva challenges that assumption. Without her, the committee risks techno-optimism bias — debating *how* to use AI safely without ever confronting the question of *whether* structured use survives serious scrutiny.

**Composition notes**:

Key productive tensions:
- **Reva vs. Vic**: The critical confrontation. Both demand evidence, but Reva's evidence set is asymmetric (strong on harms, absent on alternatives). Vic will press for evidence on both sides.
- **Reva vs. Frankie**: Values collision. Both make values arguments — Reva that AI use causes harm, Frankie that responsible use serves human flourishing. Hardest argument in the deliberation.
- **Reva + Maya**: Unexpected alliance on corporate untrustworthiness. Diverge on implication (Maya: use carefully; Reva: don't use).
- **Reva + Joe**: Alliance on historical precedent for technology failures. Diverge on whether past adaptation predicts future adaptation.
- **Reva vs. Tammy**: Reva sees a straightforward risk-benefit calculation; Tammy traces feedback loops of both using and not using AI.
