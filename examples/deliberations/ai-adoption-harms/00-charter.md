# Charter: AI Engagement Framework Deliberation

**Charge**: Given the documented harms of unstructured AI use and the claimed benefits of structured use, what is the appropriate framework for AI engagement by informed practitioners?

**Why this is hard**: The precautionary position is substantially correct on the evidence of harm. The structured-use position claims to address those harms but lacks controlled studies demonstrating that claim. The precautionary stance may be self-sealing (foreclosing the inquiry that would test it), but that observation does not refute the substance of the objections. The committee must navigate between dismissing legitimate concerns and being paralyzed by them.
