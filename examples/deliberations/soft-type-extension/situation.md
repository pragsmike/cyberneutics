# Soft Type Theory Extension Decision

Two candidate extensions to `palgebra/soft-type-theory.md` have been produced:

1. **Conservative (current document)**: Adds §5 only — the product quantale V_5 for vector-valued rubric scores, with collapse functor to scalar V at pipeline boundaries. Promotes three former open questions (coend, sheaf, Gärdenfors) to better-developed open question entries but does not treat them formally.

2. **Full treatment (v2)**: Adds §§5–7 — the product quantale (same as conservative), plus the coend construction for computing with unknown type decompositions (§6), plus a sheaf condition analysis conjecturing the presheaf is generically not a sheaf (§7). Updates open questions to reflect partially resolved items and new questions arising from the extensions.

The question is which version should become the canonical document, given the risks of hollow formalization vs. the value of advancing the formal development.
