Deep Research Prompt: Communication with Absent Parties in Cybernetics and Adjacent Fields
Search for scholarly and substantive work connecting cybernetics, second-order cybernetics, conversation theory, or systems theory to the problem of communication with absent, unreachable, or non-responsive parties. This includes but is not limited to:

Nuclear semiotics and long-term warning design: The WIPP Human Interference Task Force (Sandia, 1981-1993), the "Landscape of Thorns" designs, Thomas Sebeok's proposal to create an "atomic priesthood," Vilém Flusser's work on post-literacy. How have systems thinkers and semioticians approached the problem of encoding durable warnings without a feedback channel?
Gordon Pask's conversation theory and its boundary conditions: Does Pask or any Paskian scholar address what happens when conversation is impossible — one-way communication, deep temporal gaps, non-overlapping conceptual frameworks? Look for work by Bernard Scott, Paul Pangaro, Diana Laurillard, Nick Green, or other conversation theory inheritors on this limit case.
Decipherment as inverse hermeneutic problem: Work connecting the decipherment of unknown writing systems (Linear B, Voynich manuscript, undeciphered scripts) to cybernetic or systems-theoretic frameworks. How does the decoder reconstruct the absent sender's conceptual structure? Look for connections to Pask's entailment mesh, Bateson's "difference that makes a difference," or von Foerster's eigenform.
Anamaria Berea's work specifically: Her Folger Shakespeare Library / "literary genome" project, any published work on the Voynich manuscript, and the ASPIRE ONE lunar archive project. How does her computational social science approach handle the absent-party problem?
Technosignatures and SETI as communication theory: Work framing the search for extraterrestrial intelligence as a cybernetic or hermeneutic problem rather than purely a signal detection problem. Berea has published in this area. Also look for work by Douglas Vakoch (METI), Gonzalo Munévar, or others connecting SETI to semiotics or interpretation theory.
Information cascades without source feedback: The intermediate case where a message originator is effectively absent from downstream interpretation. Work connecting social media misinformation dynamics, rumor propagation, or information cascade models to the absent-sender framing. DARPA SocialSim program is relevant context.
Adjacent traditions: Gadamer's hermeneutics (horizon fusion when one horizon is frozen in time), Derrida's différance and the absent author, Ricoeur's distanciation (text as discourse freed from its originating situation), Eco's "open work" and limits of interpretation. Any of these connected explicitly to cybernetics or systems thinking.
Biosemiotics boundary cases: Cross-kingdom and cross-temporal signaling — how do organisms "communicate" with future generations through genetic encoding, epigenetic markers, or niche construction? Connections to Barbieri, Hoffmeyer, or Pattee on the code/matter distinction.
