### Multi-subagent committee deliberation: essays coherence audit [this session, not yet committed]

The major work. Goal: test whether the essay collection is real intellectual substance or LLM-generated plausible mishmash, and generate an improvement plan.

**Experimental setup.** Each of the five roster characters (Maya, Frankie, Joe, Vic, Tammy) was run as a **fresh Claude subagent** per round, with only their roster entry from `agent/roster.md`, the charter, a propensity-tailored reading list, and (for Round 2+) prior-round transcripts. No access to the orchestrator's conversation context. This is the agent-independence experiment referenced in `research-programs/agent-independence.md` and `research-programs/multi-model-committee.md`, implemented with the Agent tool rather than custom Python orchestration. The prior `wild/archive/subagent-personas-for-debate/README.md` notes this produces hub-and-spoke rather than true P2P; the implementation reflects that limit.

**Rounds run.** Round 1: five parallel opening statements (600-900 words each). Round 2: five parallel cross-examinations + improvement proposals (700-1000 words each). Chair (orchestrator) synthesized tensions and wrote the resolution. Independent review (`/review` skill) scored 10/15 — below threshold. Remediation round: two targeted subagents (Maya on the five-subagent meta-assumption; Tammy on Frankie's retrofit challenge) + chair-fetched verbatim quotes closing the evidence-discipline gap. Re-review scored 13/15 — at threshold.

**Verdict (post-remediation, caveated).** The essay collection contains *coherent intellectual work by Claude-trained evaluative standards*. The four-pillar synthesis (Dervin + von Foerster + Deleuze + Pask + distributional semantics) composes; the palgebra isomorphically formalizes pipeline structure. But whether that coherence constitutes intellectual substance in a cross-tradition sense, or sophisticated pattern-matching within Claude's evaluative frame, is not established and cannot be established by the committee as currently constituted. External human review from non-Claude-trained traditions is the named test.

**Durable plan.** Written to [`agent/essays-coherence-remediation-plan.md`](essays-coherence-remediation-plan.md). Four tracks: **P0** (7 scope-narrowing + prior-art edits, ~2-3 hrs), **P1** (7 self-application + case-material + bounded-claim additions, sprint-scale), **P2** (3 research commitments — outcome-tracking protocol, confidence-monotonicity-in-practice, Committee Advantage study), and **external-tradition review** (commission three scholars from outside the Claude-shaped evaluative frame).

## Key findings worth carrying forward

1. **The multi-subagent committee setup works — and has a named limit.** Running each character as a fresh Claude subagent produced genuine disagreement, earned position updates, and surfaced specific textual evidence. Tammy conceded substantively to Vic in Round 2 (a concession a single-context run would likely have smoothed over). Maya's remediation prosecuted the committee's own epistemology and pushed the score from 10 to 13. The limit: five subagents of the same base model share training-shaped priors on what counts as "intellectual substance." The committee cannot distinguish "substance by Claude's standards" from "substance simpliciter." This limit is now recorded in the resolution and in the durable plan.

2. **Editorial-review skill is live.** `/editorial-review` runs the seven-dimension audience-experience rubric against the essays and produces a structured report + prioritized remediation plan. Use after any substantive content addition or periodically (~4-6 weeks).
