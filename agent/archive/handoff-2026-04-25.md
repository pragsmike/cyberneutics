## Key findings worth carrying forward

1. **Residuality theory has multiple primary philosophical anchors across its arc, not one.** The 2021 *Philosophy* paper anchors on Serres (via Brown 2002) and Latour. The 2023 *Residuality and Representation* paper anchors on Deleuze (with Cisney, Williams, *Difference and Repetition*), Cilliers, and Kant. Both readings are correct; the multi-anchor structure is a feature of the corpus, not a contradiction. Essay-13 should reflect this, not pick one. The 2026-04-20 diary's recommendation to "soften the Deleuze framing in essays 06 and 10" was bounded to the 2021 paper and is **retracted**; existing essays 06 and 10 framings are well-supported by the 2023 paper.

2. **Three Deleuze-Guattari concepts are operative in residuality theory even where the vocabulary is absent.** Assemblage maps onto residue; rhizome maps onto the hypernetwork of residues; nomadic distribution maps onto the architectural walk. The mappings are one-to-one rather than analogical. This is a substantive intellectual finding — surfaced by mg pushing back on my own initial under-reading — and is the sharpest available frame for what cyberneutics and residuality have in common at the methodology level. See [`assemblage-rhizome-nomad.md`](../wild/residuality-theory/assemblage-rhizome-nomad.md).

3. **Cyberneutics has things to offer back to residuality theory beyond philosophical convergence.** The most concrete is the fan operation as a structured stressor-list generator (a tool residuality theory's papers describe needing without providing). Other candidate contributions: the local/global eigenform distinction, inspectable reasoning records as criticality evidence at the deliberation layer, the Naur bridge, the calibration register as long-run Ri. See [`cyberneutics-and-residuality.md`](../wild/residuality-theory/cyberneutics-and-residuality.md) §2 for the full list.

4. **Essay-13 is closer to drafting than the current README suggests, but Stacey is still in flight.** mg has ordered Stacey's *Complexity and Organizational Reality* (delivery expected late April / early May 2026); until it arrives, the cybernetics-critique answer that essay-13 must make load-bearing remains drafted only in the diary, not in source-engaged form. The other prerequisites (sit-with-paper, working notes complete, Deleuze framing corrected) are met.

5. **Essay-13 may want to be a cluster, not a single essay.** The bilateral note and fan-as-stressor-generator work as Cyberneutics-internal scaffolding and practitioner-facing artefact respectively; the assemblage/rhizome/nomad note is genuine intellectual work that could either underwrite essay-13 or stand as its own contribution. The "essay-13 is the graduation target" framing should not be read as "everything graduates as one essay."

## Theoretical notes

- **The convergence-as-narrative-proof framing.** The bilateral note and assemblage-rhizome-nomad both name this pattern: residuality and cyberneutics arrived at structurally-similar architectural commitments through independent practice (residuality from software architecture, cyberneutics from sense-making and committee deliberation), and the convergence is itself epistemic strengthening. This is the candidate spine for essay-13. A successor agent considering essay-13 should engage with this framing first.
- **Deleuze-Guattari as the unifying grammar.** The assemblage/rhizome/nomad mapping suggests that residuality and cyberneutics are not just structurally similar; they share a *conceptual ontology*. Residue is not "like" assemblage; residue *is* an assemblage in D-G's sense. This makes the convergence sharper than analogy — both methodologies operate inside the same Deleuze-Guattarian conceptual space.
- **The cybernetics critique remains an open commitment.** O'Reilly's critique of Beer-style VSM in the 2021 *Philosophy* paper applies to first-order cybernetics, not the second-order line cyberneutics inherits from. The bilateral note drafts the answer (§2.5); essay-13 must make it load-bearing. Without an explicit answer, a careful reader of both bodies of work will see a tension that cyberneutics has inherited but not addressed.
