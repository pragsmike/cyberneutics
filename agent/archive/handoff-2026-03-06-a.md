## The Conceptual Error and Its Root Causes

The editorial review agent treated "game within a game" as a metaphor (listed alongside "pachinko" and "charts on a manifold") and discussed it only in the context of committee deliberation. Two causes:

1. **The review prompt explicitly categorized it as a metaphor.** The "Metaphor hygiene" bullet in §5 listed "game within a game" in a parenthetical alongside actual metaphors. The reviewer took the instruction literally.

2. **The committee dominated word count.** In both Essay 02 and stochastic-imps, the abstract principle got a sentence or two; the committee got pages. An LLM performing editorial review weights concepts by text volume and identified the concept with its most elaborated instance.

**Neither cause was in the essays themselves.** The essays did state the broader meaning — especially stochastic-imps lines 197–199 ("making your own rules in a world that won't give you permission") and Essay 02 line 53 ("exactly what biological life does to cheat entropy"). But these statements lacked structural weight relative to the committee material.

## Theoretical/Conceptual Notes

- **The session's core insight**: An LLM performing editorial review will identify a concept with its most text-heavy instantiation. When an abstract principle (game within a game) and its concrete implementation (adversarial committee) are tightly coupled in the text, a reviewer focused on editorial concerns — navigation, tone, audience paths — will fuse them. The fix is structural: give the abstract principle its own textual weight (section headings, non-overlapping examples, bridging sentences) so that collapsing it into the implementation requires actively ignoring material.

- **This is a general vulnerability of LLM-as-reviewer.** Any concept that has one dominant example and insufficient structural separation from that example will be collapsed by an editorial reviewer. The Conceptual Hierarchy bullet added to the review prompt addresses this for future reviews, but the underlying dynamic — LLMs weight by text volume — is worth remembering for any agent task involving conceptual fidelity.

- **The "game within a game" now has a three-essay arc.** Essay 02 establishes the principle with non-LLM examples → stochastic-imps gives it a section heading and forward-references the Tilt Sound Collective → tilt-sound-collective-story makes the taxonomy of multiple games explicit. This arc is stronger than any single essay would be alone.
