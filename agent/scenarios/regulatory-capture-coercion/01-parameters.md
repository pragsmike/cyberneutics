# Parameters: Regulatory Capture Coercion

## Divergence Axes

**Visibility of the coercion** captures whether the situation remains an internal organizational crisis or becomes a public event. This is the most consequential fork: a hidden coercion forces Glenda to navigate alone, while an exposed coercion activates other institutional actors (press, judiciary, legislature, public opinion) — for better or worse.

**Institutional resilience** captures whether the broader institutional environment provides checks on Crock's regulatory power or whether the capture propagates. Regulatory capture in one agency may be an isolated failure (checked by courts, investigative journalism, or legislative oversight) or the leading edge of a systemic degradation where multiple institutions are compromised or ineffective.

These two axes produce four quadrants:

| | Coercion remains hidden | Coercion becomes public |
|---|---|---|
| **Institutions hold** | Quiet institutional response — behind-the-scenes correction | Public confrontation with institutional backing |
| **Institutions fail** | Silent erosion — Glenda complies, nobody notices | Exposed but unchecked — everyone sees the problem, nobody can fix it |

Each quadrant represents a genuinely different future with different strategic implications for Glenda's organization.
