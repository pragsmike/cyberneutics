### Procedural innovation: isolated fresh-context character agents

The remediation round was executed by spawning **five subagents in parallel**, one per roster member, each with their own context window and no awareness of the others' positions. Each character independently:
- Responded to all 5 evaluator recommendations (accept/reject/amend in voice).
- Self-classified their own analytical claims using Vic's evidence taxonomy.
- Pressure-tested the contribution-back candidates from their propensity.
- Either changed their vote or attached new conditions.

The synthesis I performed afterward preserved the disagreements rather than smoothing them. **This is the first time this repo has used isolated fresh contexts for a remediation round; it materially worked** — three sustained dissents survived synthesis, three factual self-corrections happened (Joe checked his own "~3 working notes" claim and corrected it to 5; Tammy acknowledged her "30,000+ words" was asserted before computing; Maya withdrew two of her own claims), and one position reversal under pressure (Vic withdrawing his earlier n=2 concession after Frankie's reformulation).

This procedural pattern is worth carrying forward for future remediation rounds. The single-context committee-skill default produces the homogeneity that the original deliberation suffered from. Isolation breaks the consensus loop.

## Mistakes and Lessons

- **The original committee deliberation produced premature unanimity**, scoring 10/15 on /review. Five members converged on a 15-item shared list with no sustained dissent. Lesson: when running `/committee` in single-context mode, the chair-synthesis dynamic produces convergence even when the propensities should diverge. **For remediation rounds, use isolated fresh-context character agents** (Agent tool with general-purpose subagent_type, one per roster member, parallel invocation). The procedure is documented in this session's transcript.
- **The original committee dropped "narrative proof" framing too quickly.** Vic accepted the n=2 charge in the first deliberation; Maya's cultural-overlap analogy was treated as supporting evidence. The /review evaluation correctly flagged that "narrative proof" had not been verified as undefended. The Phase 1 evidence check found the term IS defined (in [`wild/diary/2026-04-03-narrative-proof.md`](../wild/diary/2026-04-03-narrative-proof.md)) with formal readings, and is used in essay-form ([`essays/primacy-of-reasoning-artifacts.md`](../essays/primacy-of-reasoning-artifacts.md)). Lesson: **before recommending that an existing concept be dropped, locate the concept's canonical definition.** Use Grep across the essays/, wild/diary/, and palgebra/ trees as a precaution.
- **The committee accepted Tammy's six new contribution candidates with one challenge** in the original deliberation. The remediation forced honest tier classification: 2 removed, 1 promoted, 1 held, 2 reclassified as research directions about cyberneutics' practice. Lesson: when a single character adds candidates to a list, **the other characters' propensities should be applied to each candidate** even if the additions feel like progress. The fresh-context isolation pattern surfaced this naturally.
- **Joe's "~3 working notes when essay 11 was drafted" was wrong by ~40%.** Actual count was 5. Lesson: **factual claims in adversarial deliberation should be checked against the file system, not asserted from memory.** The `git log`/grep verification is cheap; the credibility cost of an asserted-wrong claim is large.
- **The 14-vs-11 internal inconsistency was missed by the remediation.** Tammy's fresh-context response corrected "fourteen subdirectories" to "11 active topic directories"; Joe's Round 2 institutional-rate claim (built on the pre-correction figure: "2 of 14") was not propagated. The /review caught this in evaluation 2. Lesson: **when one character corrects a figure, propagate the correction across linked claims in the synthesis.** I did not catch this because I synthesized the five fresh-context responses sequentially, not symmetrically.
