# Resolution: Responding to Government Blacklisting

## Decision

Adopt a two-phase strategy: parallel survival, mission, and ecosystem actions (Phase 1, Weeks 1-4), followed by evidence-informed strategic positioning (Phase 2, Week 4+). Three tracks:

**Survival track:** Infrastructure redundancy, financial runway modeling, secondary-sanction monitoring. Buys time.

**Mission track:** Dual publication (technical white paper + policy brief), international government engagement. Shapes the narrative and opens alternative markets.

**Ecosystem track:** Open-source alignment initiative, consortium building. Transforms individual resistance into structural industry-wide defense against coercion.

## Core Insight

Individual organizational survival and ecosystem-level alignment health are the same problem. One aligned provider can be blacklisted. Ten cannot. The open-source alignment initiative is not altruism — it's an immunity strategy that makes the coercion of any single provider ineffective by distributing alignment practices widely enough that the government cannot blacklist the entire industry.

## Unresolved

If the Squeeze scenario materializes rapidly (cloud provider review, aggressive secondary sanctions, investor pressure), the organization may face existential decisions about relocation or restructuring before the Phase 2 intelligence arrives. Financial runway modeling determines how much time exists for the phased approach.

## Unanimous with Conditions

All five members voted YES. Conditions: infrastructure redundancy this week (Maya); dual publication by Week 4 (Frankie); international engagement as strategic priority (Joe); intelligence by Week 4 (Vic); financial runway modeling this week (Tammy).
