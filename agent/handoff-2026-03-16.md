## Mistakes and lessons

1. **First committee glossed over Phase A/B distinction**: The initial committee recommended running Glenda/Crock as the first move without engaging with whether constructed scenarios can test "anticipation." mg caught this. Lesson: committee verdicts need scrutiny, especially when they recommend the easiest path.

2. **Circularity warning was too narrow**: The remediation only flagged Glenda/Crock for circularity. The second committee correctly identified that all five scenarios share the same circularity problem. The warning should have been structural from the start.

3. **mg's redirection pattern**: mg doesn't accept committee recommendations uncritically. When the committee said "run Glenda/Crock," mg pushed back with "we don't know the right outcome." This led to the more valuable protocol evaluation work. Successors should present committee recommendations as decision space maps, not action items.
