   - The furry logic thread connects: type membership as measurement (not declaration) is exactly what decipherers do — "this glyph probably maps to this phoneme" is a rubric-scored type assignment with calibrated uncertainty
   - Promises as the everyday case of absent-party communication (encoding intent into a structure hoping the future interpretive community will decode faithfully)
