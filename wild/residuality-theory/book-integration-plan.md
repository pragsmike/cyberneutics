## What the Phase 0 research changed

Before the per-phase changes, six findings from the research report deserve to be called out as material to the plan as a whole:

1. **Thesis is announced but not located.** No institutional repository entry, DOI, supervisor, or open-access PDF was found. O'Reilly identifies as "PhD Student in Complexity Science" (2022 talk) and as "researcher … based in Stockholm in Sweden" (2024). The thesis remains forthcoming, and the survey should reflect that the corpus is open *and* that the empirical claims are gated on a thesis nobody outside O'Reilly's circle has yet read. Stockholm signals a Swedish institution (KTH, Stockholm University, Mälardalen, Linnaeus, or one of the polytechnics) but no specific affiliation is confirmed in the search results.
2. **Reviews are sparse but real, and the criticism is substantive.** Goodreads is the only public cluster. Sympathetic reviews praise accessibility and the bridging of philosophy/complexity/architecture; critical reviews call the ideas "very abstract and not rigorous," call the book "half baked," and object to its tone toward developers ("references software developers as some sort of immature people"). Eric Normand's external read is sympathetic but criticises the *name* ("It's a terrible name"). This is enough to write the reception subsection without overclaiming consensus.
3. **The "statistically significant" experimental claim is unsourced in public.** The book asserts it; no separate methods/results paper is publicly available. The 2022 Procedia paper does not contain the underlying study. This is a *real* gap in the corpus and §10 (Limits) should treat it as such — sympathetically (the thesis may contain it) but honestly (it is not yet open to inspection).
4. **The "longer version" book has not appeared.** No revised edition, no traditional publisher imprint. The 2024 Leanpub remains the only book-length artefact. Revise the survey's forward-looking claim about a longer version: the promise stands but is unfulfilled as of search date.
5. **Continued teaching activity.** VirtualDDD ("An Introduction to Residuality Theory" and "Practical Residuality"), Avanscoperta workshops (transcribed), and software-architektur.tv interviews show O'Reilly actively teaching the methodology in 2023–2026. The "Practical Residuality" session involves "hands on work with matrices." This material is useful for the §11 / book section's "current state of practice" framing.
6. **Black Tulip Technology is named.** The 2021 Cutter PDF identifies O'Reilly as founder of Black Tulip Technology and creator of Antifragile System Design. This is biographical-affiliation grounding for the bibliography entry.

## Scope decision

**Integration strategy: option (b).** Book material integrated throughout the survey in survey-register prose (third-person, neutral, paper-grounded with book as supplementary citation). Not an appendix. Not a separate document. The survey becomes "papers + book" as a unified body.

Filename and title preserved (`state-of-residuality-2026.md`); the 2026 date refers to the cyberneutics-side survey date, not the corpus date.
